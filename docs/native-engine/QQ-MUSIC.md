# Native QQ Music subsets

Version 0.196.0 supports six frozen subsets: songs, albums, singers, playlists, charts and music videos. Modern ryqq/ryqq_v2 and bounded legacy song/album/singer/video aliases are parsed. Canonical numeric song archives are distinct from video archives; MID aliases first resolve matching track metadata.

The native client uses first-party observed UniformRuleCtrl track metadata, album/singer song-list, custom playlist, period-bound chart, EVkey URL, VideoData and MvUrlProxy calls. It does not synthesize signed URLs or bypass login/region/payment decisions. Audio grants must match the requested MID and metadata's media-file MID. Issuer-provided origins and paths are validated, preview filenames and empty/denied grants remain unavailable, and complete source decoding/duration checks protect outputs. Video grants retain only offered clear resources; permission and protection descriptors do not become ready media.

Inventories use bounded counts and actual progress, including short intermediate pages. Album/singer membership, custom playlist identity, chart rank/detail order and period are checked. Missing durations remain unavailable source positions rather than dropping rows or fabricating complete songs. All inspected selections revalidate membership, retain the inspected output position and fetch fresh selected playback. HTTPS clear grants use the shared bounded renewal contract, and recorded MID aliases avoid unnecessary playback grants after resolving their archive identity.

Public current-candidate observations verify two album entries, 921 singer entries including two unavailable rows, 146 custom playlist entries and 300 period-bound chart entries. Guest audio returned the service's login/rights refusal; the sampled video was also denied. No public QQ media-transfer success is claimed. Complete clear song/video transfers and renewal, including output/archive/callback protection, are independently generated-media verified. See MUSIC-FAMILIES.md.

Authenticated/private/subscription/region flows, additional song types/encodings, lyrics/richer metadata, other URL/filter/client layouts and longer renewed sessions remain pending. Real-account verification remains user-deferred. References: first-party [QQ Music](https://y.qq.com/) pages, current client bundle/service envelopes and denied grants captured in `work/music-families-batch/reference/`. No third-party extractor implementation was used.
