# Native NetEase music subsets

Version 0.196.0 implements seven frozen subsets: songs, albums, artists/singers, playlists, radio programs, individual programs and music videos. Canonical numeric URLs and bounded desktop fragment aliases resolve to stable archives. Song, program and video archive namespaces remain distinct; programs bind their actual main-song stream without turning its song ID into the program's archive identity.

Public legacy/current metadata and player URL, complete album, artist-song, track-ID playlist and radio APIs are parsed independently. Every selected resource checks API identity and the offered playback descriptor. Artist/radio count, ownership and continuation state are pinned. Playlist track IDs provide authoritative order; missing details are batched and unavailable positions are retained. No inventory requests player grants or media. Inspected members revalidate complete membership and fetch fresh selected playback; changed identities, denial, cancellation or short media protect prior output/archive/callback state.

Only returned clear resources are offered. Null/denied URLs, trial descriptors, protected encodings and absent clear video resources remain unavailable. All complete media uses native transfer and full local verification; clear HTTPS resource generations renew through the shared provider session. Public HTTP URLs can be transferred anonymously as returned, without upgrading or fabricating their addresses; proactive/renewed HTTP coverage remains pending.

Public current-candidate observations verify 1,841 artist songs, a 200-track playlist and 155 radio programs. Earlier album metadata succeeded, but the current guest album endpoint and one recheck returned -462 with a phone-binding requirement. This is an explicit account boundary, not a successful current guest album workflow. Sampled song and video playback remained unavailable. A separate public program completed as full MP3, retained its original and repeated without new callbacks; both files fully decode and every coded packet matches. Complete album and all other positive/negative routes are generated-media verified. See MUSIC-FAMILIES.md for evidence and exact counts.

Account/phone-binding/payment/region/protection and private workflows, other URL/filter forms, lyrics/richer metadata, more encodings and longer sustained sessions remain pending. Real-account evidence remains user-deferred; no account or browser profile was read. References are first-party [NetEase Music](https://music.163.com/) pages and public client/API captures in `work/music-families-batch/reference/`; no third-party extractor code or derived-CDN workaround was used.
