Wistia media/iframe URLs now resolve to the public embedded-media response. The C# reader validates hashed identity and bounded assets, reads available streaming containers, dimensions, sizes, bitrate, codec, title, description, and thumbnail. It does not infer the container of an unspecified original `.bin` asset. Share-slug resolution, additional legacy/incomplete/registration channel variants, additional caption/transcript/API/automatic/live layouts, access/password workflows, remaining asset types, and full parity remain pending.

JW media/playlist delivery endpoints and unsigned player aliases now resolve through C#. Signed queries on direct delivery endpoints are preserved. Source parsing retains available progressive/HLS/DASH media and captions, excludes nonempty DRM descriptors, and keeps per-entry playlist snapshots. Empty DRM objects do not incorrectly hide clear sources. New site-scoped playback endpoints, signed player resolution, additional paged/feed/search contracts, chapters, remaining track/source variations, authentication, and full parity remain pending.

Generic HTML discovery recognizes supported iframe/embed URLs, JW scripts, Wistia async/custom-player tags, and Dailymotion script attributes. It delegates only to known registered providers, bounds embed counts and recursion, and does not execute page scripts. A single embed supplies the child's media metadata. Multiple embeds form a playlist with resolved metadata and retained per-entry failures. Related DRM-capability text in an article does not prevent validation of a clear child stream. Generic-only nested frames, additional platforms/patterns, redirects, nested collections, and dynamic browser-only media still need work.
